Nothing But Love

2023-03-01T10:38:00.006+00:00https://www.tvcabbage.co.uk/2018/10/oz-tvcabbge-nothing-but-love.html

Yep, still blind. Well, it's partially sighted now so I got a downgrade and what a great hole that put me in. It's taken a few years but I'm finally accepting the fact and learning to live with my new aspects of reality. The world hasn't changed at all. It's just my part in ii has diminished to such an extent that I can't even read a recipe unless I get it up on BBC Recipes and enlarge the font to effing massive and get my magnifying glass out. At least I still have one third visual acumen in one eye and this is beginning to be accepted by my brain as all I have so I'd better make the most of it. Or else? or else sit at home frightened to venture out. Fearful of everything. Wallowing in my forever present tense of diminished visual input.

There's nothing but love now. Love of the tiny achievements. Love of rediscovering that actually my life is still there admittedly without reading books or experiencing cinema or driving. oh, all that is merely blah now. Living in a world where I dare to venture outside in daylight only has robbed me of my nightlife. A time I used to really feel alive. Daylight. Sunshine. I can get about independently and slowly. So slowly. No driving just disabled bus or the generous unasked for support of my partner and her beautiful car. A mode of transport I took for granted every day since I was 17. I'm 62 now and what a loss that was. I now run on slow blind time and that is the hardest adjustment. Not being free. Independent. Learning to be dependent and grateful for every ounce of help I get.

As far as DJing goes I cannot see the gaps between record tracks so luckily I went digital in 2002 and have a Denon Prime Go which goes with me everywhere. Failing that, a memory stick with my latest set on serves me adequately. The vinyl Nazis still mock and rip the good natured piss out of my choice of delivery method but my listening capacity remains undiminished and my ear for a good tune everyone else has missed is still strong. Nothing but love motivates me there and keeps me sane and grounded.

Support from fellow DJ's has saved me here. Support from close friends is strong and I thrive on their energy. Even support from my culture what with money I now get, money that used to come from work a teacher. I felt so overwhelmed by it all for so long it was like the real facts of life were crushing me down, but I can't give in to that. I now have nothing but love to receive and give. I don't know what they mean when they say I'm acerbic and sharp? I hope I lose none of that. But I have. A bit.
