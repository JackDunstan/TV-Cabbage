Film – Loving Vincent (2017)

Budget
$5.5 million

Box office
$30.3 million

A loving tribute to the story of Vincent Van Gogh. Told with each frame lavishly hand pinted by over 100 artists in the Van Gogh style. The simple, yet sad tale of his death told a in conventional narrative format in which the son of a friend of van Gogh tries to deliver a letter. Not challenging at all but a real visual treat nevertheless.
