Film -
Upstream Color (2013)

The water before you is somehow special.

When you drink it you feel revivied.

A tough
watch. But I like tough. Asked many questions. Like an angst ridden Terrence
Malick [sorry, lazy I know]. Intriguing.

No car
chases, explosions, no CGI, remarkably wordless. There are not many but every
word there is means something. Violation, occupation, recovery; all wrapped up
in sunshine and beauty and repetition. Great Lynchian soundscape by director Shane Carruth.

Sci–fi
does not have to be Beam me up Scotty and time loop paradoxes.
