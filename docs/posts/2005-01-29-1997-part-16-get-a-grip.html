2005-01-29T13:52:00.000+00:00https://tvcabbage.blogspot.com/2005/01/1997-part-16-get-grip.html

Today is blazing, full on, hot sunshine kind of day. It is great. Lisa has just rang and she’s going to come round and take me down to the beach for a beer. Nick is at work and all I’m doing is seethingly reliving 10 year old memories for my shitty little blog that I keep fastidiously so I don’t lay any of my heavy shit on any unsuspecting person who may or may not find it or me, offensive. Jesus! Get a grip fella!
