I love Sarah?

2006-04-11T14:24:00.000+01:00https://tvcabbage.blogspot.com/2006/04/i-love-sarah.html

Showed S my blog today and I wish I hadn't now. Thought I was being open and honest but turned out to have the opposite effect on her and made her cry when she read what I'd wrote about Tort. It made her realise the place in my heart which she should take has already been taken. But that's not true. I'm coming round to the fact that she likes me for some reason and that we have something here that's worth nurturing.
