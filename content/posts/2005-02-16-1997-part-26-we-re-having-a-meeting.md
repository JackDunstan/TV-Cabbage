But, today, at the Woodpeckers Country Hotel Bank Holiday we're blasting and the kids are playing and there is no R and therefore no tension anywhere in the group; except Polly, who fancies him rotten herself anyway and is a bit of a thick, speed freaky cow, and of course Nicola Jayne Wilson herself. The protagonist swirling dirvishly at the centre of this little drama. Loving every attention seeking second of it.

Later this week we’re ‘having a meeting’, which Nick is arranging and ‘all three of us are going to sit down and sort this bloody thing out!’ At last; we’re getting somewhere.

Gig wise everyone of the old Faversham crowd (who Nick hates) came down to support Rob Phelps, who played a blinder. As they say. It was good to see Warren who slagged off Guru Tosh and agreed to play at our next free party because ‘we need a few hours of techno’. Really?
