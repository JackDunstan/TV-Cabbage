It seems the combination of bad weather and the availability of cheap tents has led to them being discarded by campers at Glastonbury this year. The festival claimed putting tents in landfill is a greener alternative than the costly and labour-intensive job of trying to recycle them for further use. 

Glastonbury Festival 'Our Green Policies' says “‘A tent is for life not just for a festival’, we want people to not just buy the cheapest tent, spend a little extra and buy yourself a tent that is going to last you a lifetime of camping experiences rather than just a festival or summer. Pack up your tent and take it home. Any event with 177,550 attendees will generate significant levels of litter” and it sure did with thousands of tents left abandoned in the mud.
